How to play

A mystery player's career record is revealed one season at a time, in a random order.

Every wrong guess reveals another season of their career. Fewer seasons, more bragging rights.

1997  ■ CHI  82  29.6  5.9  4.3  1.7  0.5

Each season shows the team, games played, and per-game averages for points, rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks. Tap any column heading — or a team code — for what it means.

After each wrong guess you also get clue chips comparing your guess to the mystery player: whether they shared a team, whether they play the same position (Guard / Wing / Big), and whether the answer debuted earlier or later than your guess.

Normal: 8 guesses. Hard mode: 5 guesses, teams hidden, no clue chips.

Daily All-Time and per-decade games, or hit Practice for unlimited random players.
