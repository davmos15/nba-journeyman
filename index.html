How to play

A mystery player's career record is revealed one season at a time, in a random order.

Every wrong guess reveals another season of their career. Fewer seasons, more bragging rights.

2011  ■ SYD  22  31  9  14.2  4.1  5

Each season shows games played and per-game averages for points, rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks. Tap any column heading — or a team code — for what it means.

After each wrong guess you also get clues comparing your guess to the answer — shared team, position, and whether the mystery player debuted earlier or later.

Normal: 8 guesses. Hard mode: 5 guesses, teams hidden, no clue chips.

Daily All-Time and per-decade games, or hit Practice for unlimited random players.
