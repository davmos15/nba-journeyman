# 🏀 Journeyman — NBA

**[▶ Play now → nba-journeyman.netlify.app](https://nba-journeyman.netlify.app)**

Guess the mystery NBA player from their career — revealed one season at a time.
A daily puzzle for basketball nerds, with a twist: play the **All-Time** game, or
pick a single **decade** and test how well you know the 80s, 90s, 2000s, 2010s,
or 2020s.

Prefer footy? There's a sister game: **[Journeyman — AFL](https://footy-journeyman.netlify.app)**.

## How to play

A mystery player's career record appears one season at a time, in a random
order. Each row shows the **team**, **games played**, and per-game **points,
rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks** for that season.

- Every wrong guess reveals **another season**. The fewer seasons you need, the
  better your score.
- After each miss you get **clue chips**: whether you shared a team, whether you
  play the same position (Guard / Wing / Big), and whether the answer debuted
  earlier or later than your guess.
- **Normal:** 8 guesses. **Hard mode:** 5 guesses, teams hidden, no clue chips.

Come back every day for a fresh puzzle, share your result grid, and chase your
streak — each game mode tracks its own.

## Game modes

- **All-Time** — one daily player from the whole modern era (1980-present).
- **Decades** — a separate daily puzzle for the 80s / 90s / 2000s / 2010s /
  2020s. Stars who spanned eras show up in more than one.
- **Practice** — unlimited random players whenever you want more.
